## The method

- **Greek explained in Greek** from line one; Latin (Roman god names,
  Latin letters and numerals) is the only bridge language in the early
  chapters.
- **Margin gloss conventions**: `=` synonym or equivalence; `↔` antonym;
  `<` derivation (παιδίον < παῖς; μεσόγειος < μέσος + γῆ); `|` singular |
  plural or ending pairs; question/answer schemata as dashes
  (– τίνος; – τοῦ … / τῆς …); syntax formulas (Α τε καὶ Β = καὶ Α καὶ Β;
  οὔτε Α οὔτε Β, ἀλλὰ Γ); pictures with labels; arrows for ἐκεῖ/κάτω.
- **Metalanguage in Greek**: ἑνικός/πληθυντικὸς ἀριθμός, ἄρθρον, γένος,
  πτῶσις (ὀνομαστική, γενική, δοτική), ὑποκείμενον, κατηγορούμενον,
  κλίσις, ἔγκλισις, and grammar rules stated in Greek then tested as
  content ("ἆρα «νεανίας» γένος θηλυκόν ἐστιν ἢ ἀρσενικόν;").
- **Exercise ladder per chapter** (Μελέτημα Α/Β/Γ): fill endings → fill
  words from a word bank → 10–11 numbered comprehension questions mixing
  story and metalanguage; then ΛΕΞΕΙΣ grouped by part of speech
  (ὀνόματα, ἐπίθετα, ῥήματα, ἐπιρρήματα, ἀντωνυμίαι, σύνδεσμοι, μόρια,
  ἀριθμοί, προθέσεις, φράσεις, κύρια ὀνόματα).
- **Devices**: one new form at a time, recycled immediately in Q/A pairs;
  lettered sections Α/Β/Γ with line numbers every 5 lines; labelled
  character galleries and a family-tree diagram; habitat pictures that
  carry meaning without English; a dual pronunciation table (historical
  vs Erasmian) in the introduction.

## Cast and setting (not to be copied)

Apollonius and Helen's household with children Patroclus, Cassandra,
Hypatia, Apollodorus; grandparents Hippolytus and Pandora; slaves
Philippus, Callimachus, Electra, Callirrhoe; friends Aesop and Thaleia.
Olympians with Roman counterparts, monsters, and historical figures.
Attica and Athens from Chapter ΚΓ'.

## How the course uses it

- Our lesson template already has Ἀνάγνωσις / Παρατηρήσατε / Ἀσκήσεις /
  Ἐρωτήματα; rename the exercise block **Μελετήματα** and keep the A/B/C
  ladder (endings cloze → word-bank cloze → Greek questions) as the
  fixed spine of every lesson's exercises, before the Athenaze-style
  transformation and composition items.
- Adopt the gloss symbol set exactly (`=`, `↔`, `<`, `|`, dash schemata)
  in `Gloss` and the margin column; render them as text, not icons.
- Adopt Greek grammatical metalanguage from Unit 1 (ἑνικός, πληθυντικός,
  ἄρθρον, πτῶσις) with the English toggle showing the English term.
- Group each lesson's ΛΕΞΕΙΣ by part of speech in the vocab step.
- Our Stage 0 keeps LOGOS's "letters and numbers" and "historical vs
  Erasmian" contrast, replacing Erasmian with our Classical Attic target
  vs Modern Greek.
