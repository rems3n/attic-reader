Source: `Athenaze-Vol1-Workbook.pdf` in the user's Drive folder. The
extraction covered front matter and Chapters 1–8 only (9–16, the grammar
reviews and the answer key are missing). OCR of the Greek is poor, so
the catalogue below records shapes, not text.

## Sequencing within a chapter

Each chapter mirrors the textbook α/β halves. Within a half:
morphology drills for the new grammar (8–30 items) → syntax layer
chained onto the same items (label what you just produced) → optional
accent exercise → vocabulary pair (Greek→English then English→Greek) →
one reading to translate at chapter end. Counts rise from 11 exercises
in Ch. 1 to 14 in Ch. 5 and 8. Progression is recognition → production:
Ch. 1–2 mostly circle and multiple-choice, Ch. 3 on generate forms from
a lemma, Ch. 4–5 transformations and full-sentence production, Ch. 6–8
two-layer items (produce + label) and translate-plus-classify.

## Devices

- Readings with bracketed glosses; some glosses ask for word-building
  ("deduce from ὁ δοῦλος"; "deduce from ἀπο- and ἐλαύνω").
- Chained exercises reuse the blanks of the previous exercise.
- Accent track marked optional throughout.
- Cumulative vocabulary lists by part of speech with a self-test method.
- Not present: pictures with captions, crosswords, dictation, Greek
  comprehension questions (comprehension is tested only by translation).

## How the course uses it

- The 17 exercise types in `COURSE_PLAN.md` §4.1 already cover most of
  these; add the ones we lacked: **chained labelling** (produce a form,
  then code its use), **parallel-paradigm transfer**, **endings cloze
  passage**, **article-for-change-of-subject**, **elision rewrite**, and
  a **function-labelling** parse (S / DO / IO / linking verb).
- Adopt the per-half sequencing: morphology → syntax layer → accent
  (optional) → vocabulary pair → reading.
- Keep the optional accent track as our "lenient until Unit 4" policy.
- We add what the workbook lacks: pictures, dictation, Greek questions.
