## How the transition to real texts is handled

- Ch. 17–19: family story in α/β; only the tail reading is adapted
  Herodotus. Ch. 20: Thucydides tails begin. Ch. 21β: first classical
  text inside a chapter (Pericles). From Ch. 22 tails are near-verbatim;
  Ch. 23 is entirely Thucydides. Ch. 24 pivots through Plato to Herodotus
  (25–28), then Thucydides (29–30), then Aristophanes (31).
- Spelling policy: Attic in the family story; Thucydidean (ἐς, -σσ-,
  ξυν-) kept in Thucydides passages and their exercises, with the Attic
  spelling first in vocab lists; Ionic kept from Ch. 24 tail on.
- Glossing: non-core glossed words are not in the chapter vocabulary and
  are not tested. Constructions are previewed in glosses several chapters
  before they are taught (indirect statement from Ch. 17, optative in
  Ch. 19, pluperfect in Ch. 23). After each grammar section learners go
  back into the passage to find the forms. Illustration captions
  pre-teach each chapter's new construction. Aspect is stressed over
  tense for participles. Duals are excluded entirely.

## Exercise types (Book II)

Form production pairs/triples (future/aorist, subjunctive→optative);
parse-and-translate lists; Greek→English on the new syntax;
English→Greek; paired model sentences (translate the Greek, then render a
parallel English sentence); fill-in relative with attraction; a
**continuation composition** after every tail reading, in the story's
context and dialect; Word Study and Word Building every chapter;
locate-in-text tasks. New in Book II: full principal parts after each
reading, grouped by stem type (labial, guttural, dental, liquid,
nasal-infix, -σκω, -μι, εἰ-augment).

## Vocabulary, review, testing

Full principal parts for most verbs; already-known verbs reappear only to
show parts. "New usage of preposition" flags per chapter. Explicit
page-referenced review of Book I before new material. No statements about
tests or quizzes in the handbook.

## How the course uses it

- Stage 2 (Units 7–12) in `COURSE_PLAN.md` mirrors the Ch. 17–25 grammar
  order: future and εἶμι → -μι verbs → genitive absolute and article →
  ἵστημι and supplementary participles → subjunctive → fear/indefinite
  clauses, indirect statement → passive → aorist passive → optative.
  The plan's order was adjusted to match (see §2.5).
- Adopt the "preview in gloss before teaching" device and the
  "find the forms in the passage" step (a `locate` exercise type).
- Adopt continuation composition as the Stage 2 free-production task.
- Our tracks (§2.6) replace Book II's Herodotus arc (Ionic; user excluded
  Herodotus) with Attic prose, but keep the Thucydides and Aristophanes
  targets and the Plato Protagoras/education theme.
